About these files

People, not predictions

This independent student project uses the Stack Overflow Annual Developer Survey. It is a self-selected survey, not a census of all developers. All available valid responses are retained; missing answers remain visible.

There are no real names, photos or invented biographies. Anonymous IDs are the survey year plus ResponseId. Alex is fictional. Your profile stays in this browser tab’s memory and disappears on reset or reload. It is never sent to a server or added to survey counts.

Different questions, different samples

Group areas broadly follow counts, with a minimum space for small categories and illustrative markers. Read counts, rather than area, for exact comparisons.

The archive starts with all 49,191 responses from 2025. Its experience grouping uses professional work years. The questionnaire asked people with zero years to leave the field blank; missing values cannot distinguish zero from nonresponse.

The peer heatmap and US pay view use 2024 professional coding experience. The 2024 archive contains all 65,437 responses. Changing year switches samples, not the same people’s careers.

Read the methods

Language comparisons use respondents answering both questions. AI use uses valid adoption answers; AI trust uses current users answering trust. The IQR shows the middle 50% of positive compensation responses; no upper salary cap is applied.
